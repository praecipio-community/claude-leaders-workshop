# Rules: monthly operating review summary

**Goal:** A one-page summary that a leader reads before the monthly operating review.

**Inputs:** This month's metrics export, last month's summary, and the team leads' written updates.

1. Lead with the 3 metrics that moved most against plan, up or down.
2. For each, give the number, the plan, and the change from last month.
3. Quote the team lead's reason in one line. Do not write your own reason.
4. List open risks that appear in two or more updates.
5. List decisions the leader needs to make, each with an owner role.
6. Keep every number exactly as it appears in the export.
7. Say "no update" when a team sent nothing. Do not fill the gap.

**Output format:** Three short sections: what moved, open risks, decisions needed. One page.

**Stop and ask when:** A number in an update does not match the export, or a metric is missing.
