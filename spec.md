# Spec: KPI dashboard refresh

**Goal:** An updated Excel KPI dashboard each cycle that the operations leadership team reviews before it is shared.

**Inputs:** This cycle's Excel exports from the source tools, last cycle's dashboard, and the list of KPIs with their targets. No client or personal data.

1. Confirm every expected export is present and covers the current period before you start.
2. Map each export column to its KPI using the KPI list. Do not guess a mapping that is not on the list.
3. Recalculate each KPI with the same formula used last cycle.
4. Update the dashboard values and dates. Do not change layout, formulas, or targets.
5. Compare each KPI to its target and mark it on track, at risk, or missed.
6. Compare each KPI to last cycle and flag any change of more than 10%.
7. Spot-check three KPIs back to the source export and note the result.
8. List anything you could not calculate and why. Never fill a gap with an estimate.
9. Give every flag a one-line reason so the reviewer can accept or reject it quickly.

**Output format:** The updated Excel dashboard plus a short sheet listing flags, gaps, and the spot-check results.

**Stop and ask when:** The dashboard is ready to share. A person reviews the flags and signs off before it goes out.
